Candyverse: Block & Bloom

Place blocks, choose upgrades and grow your candy island. Play offline.

Place colorful shapes and let your candy island bloom.

Candyverse: Block & Bloom combines an 8×8 block puzzle with upgrades for each run and a small, cozy world to grow.

You always have three pieces. Place them on the board, complete rows and columns, earn points and plan your next move. There is no time pressure. Drag a piece with a preview, or select it and tap a board cell.

Every five cleared lines, choose an available upgrade:
• Sugar Rush boosts points for clearing lines.
• Lucky Jelly boosts coins for a completed run.
• Rainbow Eraser removes one occupied cell after confirmation.
Upgrades last only for the current game.

Earn Candy Coins by clearing lines. After a run ends, spend them on the next levels of your Candy Tree, Jelly House and Rainbow Fountain. Each upgrade changes the island's appearance. Pink Mochi, Blue Jelly, Lemon Blob and Minty Monster react to your touch.

Play offline, without accounts, ads or paid in-app purchases. Progress and your current game are saved locally. Take a break and return without a penalty for being away.

Choose Polish or English, switch sound and haptics off, or reduce motion. You can delete local data in settings after confirmation. The publisher has no cloud save; uninstalling may remove your progress.
