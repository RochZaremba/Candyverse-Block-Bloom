# Original Candyverse assets

Candy tiles, Home candy bloom, backdrop and particles are original code-native
artwork implemented for this repository. No stock art, brands or external
character designs are used. Their source uses the repository's license terms.

`audio/place.wav`, `audio/clear.wav`, `audio/upgrade.wav` are original synthesized
PCM tones (22050 Hz, mono, 16 bit), reproducible with
`scripts/generate_audio.py`. No samples or melodies were imported. The author
dedicates these three audio files to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Test-only Roboto fonts retain the Apache 2.0 license in
`test/fonts/Roboto_LICENSE.txt`. Flutter Material icons are used for standard
settings controls, not as candy game artwork.
