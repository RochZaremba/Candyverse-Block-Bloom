# Original Candyverse assets

Candy tiles, Home candy bloom, backdrop and particles are original code-native
artwork implemented for this repository. No stock art, brands or external
character designs are used. Their source uses the repository's license terms.

`audio/place.wav`, `audio/clear.wav`, `audio/upgrade.wav` are original synthesized
PCM tones (22050 Hz, mono, 16 bit), reproducible with
`scripts/generate_audio.py`. No samples or melodies were imported. Distribution licensing of original project assets remains the project owner's decision; this catalog does not make a public-domain dedication on their behalf.

Test-only Roboto fonts retain the Apache 2.0 license in
`test/fonts/Roboto_LICENSE.txt`. Flutter Material icons are used for standard
settings controls, not as candy game artwork.

The island diorama and Candy Tree/Jelly House/Rainbow Fountain at levels0–3,
and Pink Mochi/Blue Jelly/Lemon Blob/Minty Monster in
`lib/island/presentation/island_scene.dart`, are original code-native artwork
created for Candyverse. Shapes, idle and tap animation are authored in this
repository; no third-party character references, textures or emoji are used.
They follow the repository's license terms.
