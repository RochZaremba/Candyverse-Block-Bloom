# Candyverse asset provenance and licensing notes

## Current Candy Kingdom artwork (1.1.0+2)

The owner supplied the Candyverse banner/logo and kitten icon reference in
`Candyverse_ Whimsical Candy Kingdom-1.png`. The unchanged source composite is
[`kingdom/brand-sheet.png`](kingdom/brand-sheet.png); the app selects its banner
at paint time. The kitten/puppy/bunny/fox sprites, building evolutions, island
backgrounds and full-bleed kitten icon were generated with the built-in image
tool using that reference. They are raster artwork, not code-native shapes.

File-by-file provenance and processing notes are in
[`kingdom/README.md`](kingdom/README.md); the full prompts and generation settings
are in [`kingdom/PROMPTS.md`](kingdom/PROMPTS.md). The owner must confirm rights
to the supplied artwork before store submission. This catalog records sources;
it does not assert legal clearance, exclusive ownership or a public-domain
license for the supplied or generated images.

`scripts/generate_brand_icon.py` packages/resamples `kingdom/icon.png` into
`brand/store_icon_512.png`, `brand/icon_preview.png` and Android launcher density
PNGs. The adaptive icon uses that raster foreground with a solid-color background;
the Android13 monochrome silhouette is a code-authored vector. The packaging
script does not generate the kitten illustration or call an image service.
All artwork is bundled locally; no image-generation service ships in the app.

## Code-authored artwork and audio

Candy tiles, gameplay backdrop and particles remain original code-native artwork
implemented in this repository. Their source follows the repository's terms.

`audio/place.wav`, `audio/clear.wav`, `audio/upgrade.wav` and `audio/fanfare.wav`
are original synthesized PCM tones (22050 Hz, mono, 16 bit), reproducible with
`scripts/generate_audio.py`. No samples or melodies were imported. Distribution
licensing of original project assets remains the project owner's decision;
this catalog does not make a public-domain dedication on their behalf.

Test-only Roboto fonts retain the Apache 2.0 license in
`test/fonts/Roboto_LICENSE.txt`. Flutter Material icons are used for standard
settings controls, not as candy game artwork.

## Historical 1.0.0-rc.1 artwork

The published 1.0.0-rc.1 used code-native Home candy bloom, island diorama,
Candy Tree/Jelly House/Rainbow Fountain at levels0–3 and the original
Pink Mochi/Blue Jelly/Lemon Blob/Minty Monster characters. Its smiling bloom
launcher/store mark was generated from code and palette tokens without external
character references. The retained [`brand/candyverse_mark.svg`](brand/candyverse_mark.svg)
records that older mark; the current PNG exports and launcher resources use the
rendered kitten instead. Existing code-generated PL/EN feature graphics in
`store/` are historical release materials, not screenshots of Candy Kingdom.
The immutable public 1.0.0-rc.1 artifacts are not regenerated by this update.
